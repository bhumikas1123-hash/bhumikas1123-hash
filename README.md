## 🌟 Featured Project

### 🧠 Founder Customer Intelligence System
> **IIT Guwahati Summer Project 2025** · *Collaborative with Akshit Sukhija · My role: Analytics Validation & Visualization*

A founder-level D2C customer intelligence engine built for a fashion brand with **3,900+ customer records across 50 US states**. This project goes well beyond standard reporting — it answers the questions a business actually needs answered:

- 🔍 Who are genuinely loyal customers vs. those who only buy with discounts?
- ⚠️ Which segments are quietly destroying margins through promo dependency?
- 📍 Where are the strongest organic demand regions for sustainable growth?

**Full pipeline:** Raw CSV → Python feature engineering → SQL business intelligence → Power BI 4-panel dashboard

| KPI | Value |
|-----|-------|
| 🏥 Retention Health Score | 0.56 / 1.0 |
| ⚠️ Promo Dependency Risk | 0.43 — Moderate |
| 🌟 Premium Loyalists | 36.4% (1,420 of 3,863 customers) |
| 🗺️ Organic Demand Coverage | All 50 US states tracked |

**7 engineered features** including `Loyalty_Score_A`, `Loyalty_Score_B`, `Promo_Dependency_Score`, `Purchase_Intensity_Score`, and `Master_Customer_Segment` (5 strategic labels)

**5 production SQL queries:** Loyal vs Promo Buyers · Regional Organic Demand · Category Retention · Segment Spend Intelligence · Premium Loyalist Identification

> 💡 **Key finding:** 1,640 "Discount Survivors" carry 1.00 promo dependency — a sustained margin risk. Premium Loyalists show zero promo dependency — the most reliable long-term retention base.
